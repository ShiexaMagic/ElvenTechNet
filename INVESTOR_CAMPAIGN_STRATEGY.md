## 🎓 Best Practices from Venture Marketing

### ✅ DO:
- Write all subject lines WITH urgency/specificity
- Personalize each outreach (mention their thesis)
- Make ONE ask per email (page view OR meeting, not both)
- Follow up 2-3 times per prospect (week 1, week 3, week 5)
- Track everything in a spreadsheet
- Get warm intros whenever possible
- Let the page sell the features, you focus on relationship
- Create FOMO with "round capacity" mentions (if true)
- Celebrate early wins (tell other investors who's in)

### ❌ DON'T:
- Send blast emails to 500 people (quality > quantity)
- Use generic subject lines
- Make it too easy to say no
- Assume one conversation = interest (they need time)
- Share the page with everyone (exclusivity > noise)
- Make the investor page too long (it's not, keep it)
- Use hype you can't back up (Tesla is REAL proof)
- Forget the human element (email personal, not automated)

## 📞 When to Make the Ask

**ASK FOR MONEY WHEN:**
- ✅ They've reviewed the investor page
- ✅ They've asked detailed questions (shown interest)
- ✅ They've said something positive
- ✅ You've built rapport in 1-2 conversations

**DON'T ASK IF:**
- ❌ They just said "let me think about it"
- ❌ It's the first conversation
- ❌ They asked hypothetical questions
- ❌ They pivoted to due diligence requests without clear interest

**The Ask Usually Comes From THEM:**
"OK, we're interested. What are the next steps?"

**Your Job:** Be ready with the answer.
