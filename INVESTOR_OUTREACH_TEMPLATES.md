## Pro Tips for Maximum Response Rate

1. **Personalize the intro** - Mention something specific about why THIS investor should care
2. **Lead with traction** - Start with Tesla/partnerships, not the tech
3. **Create urgency** - "Last angel round" is real and powerful
4. **Make it easy** - Direct link, clear CTA
5. **One ask per email** - Either meeting or page view, not both
6. **Follow up** - Send 2-3 follow-ups in 2-week cadence
